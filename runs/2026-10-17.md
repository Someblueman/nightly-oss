**Claim and acceptance:** with `indexHeader(1)`, chunks `preamble\r` and
`\nid,note\r\n1,ok\r\n` must return `[{ id: "1", note: "ok" }]`, just as unsplit
input does. The [claim](2026-10-17/claim.md) was saved before production edits.
The baseline instead returns no rows because it counts CR and LF as two record
separators. The localized fix keeps a trailing CR in the retained buffer until
the next character or final flush resolves it. Genuine blank records still
count toward header indexing; standalone CR/LF and quoted line breaks are covered.
