**Claim and acceptance:** preserve literal braces in dictionary keys when inferring
positional format arguments, keep following argument numbers correct, and return
correct field names from tokenization. The criteria were stated before production
edits. The [written claim](2026-10-14/claim.md) was assembled after the initial
checks when the operator requested the V3R1T4S review protocol. It does not claim
file-based preregistration before that first experiment.

For example, `{[{}]} {}` with `({'{}': 'value'}, 'tail')` previously became
`{0[{1}]} {2}` and raised `KeyError`. It now becomes `{0[{}]} {1}` and formats to
`value tail`. The patch matches complete field names, preserving item-key text,
and numbers only fields with omitted argument names.
