**Claim and acceptance:** `Squeeze` must preserve the original bytes of retained
input segments while removing adjacent repeated runes selected by its pattern.
The [claim](2026-10-15/claim.md) was saved before production edits. Rune comparison
and pattern semantics stay unchanged, including how invalid bytes decode to U+FFFD.

For example, `Squeeze("\xff\xfe", "a")` previously returned `"\xff�"` even though
neither decoded rune matches `a`. The fix returns the unchanged two input bytes.
It also preserves invalid bytes after selected duplicates, such as
`Squeeze("aa\xff", "a") == "a\xff"`.
